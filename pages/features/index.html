Features

Buy Tickets

Discover and book tickets for concerts, movies, theaters, and small events - all in one place. Architix gives you an interactive seat map, letting you choose your perfect spot before checkout. Whether it’s a grand concert or a cozy gathering, buying tickets has never been this simple and visual.

Sell Tickets

Create and manage your own events with ease. Architix lets you design custom seating layouts, set prices, and track sales — all in one platform. Whether you’re hosting a concert, a play, or a small gathering, you have full control over how your audience books their seats
